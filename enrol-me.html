Enrolling in Your GroupSource Plan

New to GroupSource? Here's how to complete your enrolment online with Enrol-ME Online, from your welcome email to signing your form.

How it works What you'll need Step by step Signing your form After you enrol

How it works

When your employer joins GroupSource, you'll enrol online through Enrol-ME Online. It takes just a few minutes and replaces paper forms.

1. Your employer signs you up
They send GroupSource your basic employment details and the date your coverage starts.
2. You get a welcome email
It includes a personal link to Enrol-ME Online, plus your username and password.
3. You enrol online
Fill in your details at a time that suits you, review them and submit.
Can't find your welcome email? Check your junk or spam folder. If it isn't there, contact your plan administrator, usually your HR department.

What you'll need

Having these handy will make enrolment quick:

Your personal details, including your date of birth and address
Names and dates of birth for your spouse and any dependents you want to cover
The name of the person (or people) you want as your beneficiary

Step by step

A progress bar at the top of the screen shows where you are. Select Next to move through each stage.

1
Sign in
Select the link in your welcome email and sign in with the username and password provided.
2
Welcome
Read the welcome message, then select Next.
3
Privacy policy
Read the privacy policy and select Accept to continue.
4
Member information
Enter your details. Use your mouse or the Tab key to move between fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*), and you'll see a warning if something is missing or incorrect.
5
Spouse and dependents
Add the family members you want covered by your plan.
6
Beneficiaries
Name who should receive your benefit if you pass away.
7
Optional coverage
You'll be offered Member Advantage insurance, optional life and critical illness coverage you're pre-approved for when you first enrol. A short video explains how it works, and the cost is deducted from your paycheque. Learn more about Member Advantage.
8
Final review
Check everything you've entered, make any changes, then select Submit.

Signing your form

How you sign depends on where you live.

British Columbia and Quebec
You'll sign electronically, right after you submit. Agree to the electronic disclosures and signatures consent, review your enrolment form, then sign using your mouse or touchscreen.
Nothing to print
All other provinces and territories
Print your completed form, then sign and date it. Return it to your plan administrator, who will confirm your beneficiary designation.
Print, sign and return
Haven't finished yet? GroupSource follows up with anyone whose enrolment is still outstanding, so you may get a reminder. Completing it promptly, with accurate details, means your claims can be processed without delays.
