What is sparrow?

sparrow is GroupSource's health and wellness app. It brings mental health care, virtual doctor visits, coaching and pharmacy services together in one place, included with your benefits plan. Your history is shared across sparrow's care providers, so you don't have to repeat your story.

Who's covered
You, your spouse and your dependents
Where
One app and login on your phone or computer, in English or French
Privacy
Confidential. Your employer never sees who uses it

The services available to you depend on your plan design. Check Care Services in the app for the full list your plan includes.

Pharmacy to your door

sparrow's digital pharmacy, powered by GreenShield Pharmacy, delivers your medications free, anywhere in Canada.

Free delivery with a $7.99 dispensing fee (fees may vary by medication).
Easier medication management with auto-refills and daily dose packaging. The pharmacy team can also contact your doctor when you run out of refills.
Virtual pharmacist consultations to review your medications.
Over-the-counter products are available too, through the Health Store.

Transfer a prescription from your current pharmacy

1
In the sparrow app, open the pharmacy section and select Add or transfer prescriptions.
2
Enter your current pharmacy's details, including its address and phone number.
3
Choose to transfer all your prescriptions or only some.
4
sparrow contacts your pharmacy for you, then the pharmacy team gets in touch to talk through your medications.
Where it's available: GreenShield Pharmacy serves members in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland, Yukon and the Northwest Territories.

Medical second opinion

Facing a serious diagnosis, like cancer? sparrow can connect you with leading medical specialists to review your diagnosis and treatment options.

The service works with your treating physician and provides a full report on your diagnosis, treatment options and their impacts, so you can make confident decisions with fewer unnecessary treatments and less travel.
